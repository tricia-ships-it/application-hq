# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Tracker, deadlines view, documents checklists and essay workspace
- Guided setup: profile, interests and any number of organisations
- Personal scout prompt for any AI assistant, on fixed weekdays or custom dates
- Shift calendar: read a rota photo with an AI assistant, review it on a calendar, and turn it into scout and reminder dates
- Calendar (.ics) export with reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before each deadline, and on the day
- How-to-apply notes and essay outlines or sample drafts from imports
- Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) and JSON backup exports
- Security: no network access, script-hash Content Security Policy, sanitised imports, formula-free exports
