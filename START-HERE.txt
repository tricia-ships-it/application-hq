APPLICATION HQ: START HERE
==========================

A private tracker for fellowships, scholarships, funded events and grants.
It runs in your web browser. No account, and your data stays on your computer.

1. OPEN IT
   Unzip this folder, then double-click "index.html".
   It opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
   To make it feel like an app, in Chrome: menu (three dots) > Cast, save and share >
   Create shortcut > tick "Open as window". In Edge: menu > Apps > Install this site as an app.

2. SET IT UP (about 5 minutes)
   Click "Set up my tracker" and fill in the Setup & scout tab:
   - About you: the facts your essays and scout will use
   - What to look for: types of opportunity, topics, regions
   - Who you apply for: yourself, plus any organisations you lead
   - Your scout & reminders: when it should run (fixed weekdays, or your own
     dates; shift workers can use a photo of their rota)

3. TURN ON YOUR SCOUT
   Click "Copy scout prompt" and paste it into an AI assistant that can browse
   the web (for example Claude or ChatGPT). Ask it to set it up as a scheduled task.
   Attach your "Back up" file so it knows what you already track.
   Each run, it gives you a file. Click "Import" in Application HQ to add it.
   Your statuses and essays are never overwritten.

4. TURN ON REMINDERS
   Click "Add deadlines to my calendar (.ics)" and open the file. Your phone
   alerts you 7, 3 and 1 days before each deadline, and on the day.
   Do it again after each import.

5. APPLY
   Open any opportunity to see how to apply, the requirements, a documents
   checklist and the essay questions with outlines or sample drafts.
   Edit them into your own words, mark them Final, then copy or download
   them into the real application form.

6. KEEP IT SAFE
   Click "Back up" regularly. Clearing your browser data erases the tracker.
   Your backup file contains your personal story and plans, so keep it private.
   On a shared computer, use your own browser profile.
   Never pay to apply for a "fully funded" programme, and never give an AI
   assistant your passwords, ID numbers or bank details.
   More in SECURITY.md.

The full guide is in README.md (it opens in any text editor, or nicely on GitHub).
