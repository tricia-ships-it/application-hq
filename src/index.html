4. Your scout & reminders

Application HQ doesn't search the web itself. Your scout is an AI assistant you give the prompt below, ideally one that can browse the web and run scheduled tasks (for example Claude or ChatGPT). It finds opportunities, researches requirements and essay questions, and replies with a file you Import here. Reminders come from your own calendar: add your deadlines below and your phone alerts you 7, 3 and 1 days before.

Your shift calendar
